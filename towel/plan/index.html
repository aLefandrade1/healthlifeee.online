Before you start — please read. Talk to your doctor first if you are pregnant, gave birth in the last 12 weeks (or had a C-section), had recent abdominal or back surgery, have a hernia, osteoporosis, a diagnosed spine or disc condition, or any condition that makes lying on your back uncomfortable. A gentle stretch is normal. Stop right away if you feel sharp pain, numbness, tingling or dizziness.

How to use this plan

Less than 10 minutes a day, at home, with a bath towel you already have.

  1. Read sections 1 to 4 once (about 5 minutes).
  2. Find your belly type in section 3 — it tells you your towel spot.
  3. Every day: the towel session (section 2) + that phase's follow-up flow (section 5).
  4. Tick the day in the 21-day calendar (section 6). Your progress is saved on this phone.
Best time: morning before breakfast or at night before bed. Same time every day makes the habit stick. Wait about 2 hours after a big meal.

1 · The wall test

Your starting point. Do it on day 1, day 7 and day 21.

  1. Stand with your heels, butt, upper back and head against a wall.
  2. Slide one flat hand into the space behind your lower back.
  3. Notice how much room there is. If your whole hand fits with room to spare, your lower back is arching and your belly is being pushed forward.

2 · The towel session

The three details that most people get wrong: the towel, the spot, and what comes after.

Detail 1 · The towel
  1. Take a regular bath towel and fold it in half lengthwise.
  2. Roll it tightly into a firm cylinder about 4 inches (10 cm) thick.
  3. Wrap two hair ties or rubber bands around it so it doesn't unroll.
Sensitive back, after kids, or new to this? Start with a thinner roll (about 3 in / 7 cm — a hand towel works) for the first week.
Detail 2 · The position
  1. Sit on a mat or carpet with your legs straight. Place the rolled towel crosswise behind you.
  2. Lie back slowly so the towel sits under your back at your spot (section 3). Most women: right behind the belly button.
  3. Legs about hip-width apart. Turn your feet inward so your big toes touch and your heels stay apart.
  4. Raise your arms straight overhead to the floor, palms down, and let your pinky fingers touch. Tight shoulders? Keep your arms a little wider or your elbows slightly bent.
  5. Let your body get heavy. Breathe slowly in through the nose (belly rises) and out through the mouth (belly softens).

Detail 3 · What comes after
  1. Bring your arms down to your sides first.
  2. Bend your knees, then roll onto your side and push yourself up with your hands. Never sit straight up from the towel.
  3. Lie flat without the towel for 3 slow breaths.
  4. Do your phase's follow-up flow (section 5) — 2 to 4 minutes. This is what most videos leave out.

3 · Your belly-type adjustment

Pick the one that sounds most like you. Not sure? Use "All over".

Bloats by the end of the day

Spot: right behind the belly button. Towel: standard 4 in.

Focus: long, slow exhales during the hold (count to 6). Do your session in the evening, and add the 10-minute walk after dinner and the de-bloat tea from week 1.

Pooch below the belly button

Spot: one finger-width below the belly button. Towel: standard 4 in.

Focus: in the Realign Flow, do 15 pelvic tilts instead of 10. During the hold, let your lower back sink down — don't arch it.

Sticks out up top (under the chest)

Spot: one finger-width above the belly button. Towel: standard 4 in.

Focus: rib breathing — on each exhale, feel your lower ribs close in. Do the couch & desk posture fix every day, not just on the listed days.

After having kids

Spot: right behind the belly button. Towel: thinner 3 in roll for week 1. Holds of 1 minute until it feels easy.

Focus: on every exhale, gently draw your belly button toward your spine (about 30% effort). If you notice a ridge or gap down the middle of your belly when you lift your head, talk to your doctor or a pelvic floor physio before doing the Corset Flow.

All over

Spot: right behind the belly button. Towel: standard 4 in.

Focus: follow the plan exactly as written, including the bonuses.

5 · The follow-up flows

Right after the towel, on your mat. 2 to 4 minutes.

Days 1–7

Realign Flow

  1. Pelvic tilts · 10 reps. On your back, knees bent, feet flat. Gently press your lower back into the floor, then release.
  2. Knee hug · 30 seconds. Hug both knees to your chest and rock softly side to side.
  3. Single knee to chest · 5 breaths each side. The other leg stays bent, foot on the floor.
  4. Belly breathing · 5 breaths. Hands on your belly, slow inhale through the nose, long exhale.
Days 8–14

Corset Flow

  1. Dead bug · 8 per side. On your back, arms up, knees bent at 90°. Slowly lower the opposite arm and leg, keeping your lower back on the floor. Return and switch.
  2. Heel slides · 10 per side. Knees bent, slide one heel away along the floor and back while you exhale.
  3. Towel squeeze bridge · 12 reps. Put the rolled towel between your knees, squeeze it, and lift your hips. Lower slowly.
  4. 360° breathing · 5 breaths. Hands on your lower ribs: breathe into the sides and back of your ribs, then exhale fully.
Days 15–21

De-Bloat Flow

  1. Cat–cow · 8 slow reps. On hands and knees, round your back as you exhale, gently arch as you inhale.
  2. Lying twist · 5 breaths each side. On your back, knees bent, let both knees fall to one side.
  3. Wind-relieving pose · 30 seconds. Hug your right knee in, then left, then both.
  4. Child's pose · 5 breaths. Knees wide, forehead down, breathe into your back.
Plus a 10-minute walk after your largest meal on most days.

7 · Your gifts

Gift 1

3-minute lymphatic drainage

Do it in the morning, with light pressure — just enough to move the skin, never deep.

  1. Neck · 30 s. Flat fingers just above your collarbones: gentle circles, 10 times.
  2. Armpits · 30 s. Gently pump each armpit with a flat hand, 10 times.
  3. Belly · 60 s. Slow clockwise circles around your belly button, getting a little wider each time.
  4. Groin · 30 s. Flat hands in the creases at the top of your legs, gentle strokes upward, 10 times.
  5. Breathe · 30 s. 3 deep belly breaths, then drink a glass of water.
Gift 2

Couch & desk posture fix

  1. Sit on your sit bones. Rock side to side until you feel the two bones under your butt, and sit tall on them.
  2. Rolled towel behind your lower back when you sit for long periods (car, desk, couch).
  3. Chin nod · 5 reps. Slide your chin straight back (double chin), hold 3 seconds.
  4. Shoulder blade squeeze · 10 reps. Pull your shoulder blades down and together, hold 3 seconds.
  5. Stand up every hour and take 5 slow breaths with your hands on your lower ribs.
Gift 3

The de-bloat tea

You need: 1 cup hot water · 4–5 thin slices of fresh ginger · 1 peppermint tea bag (or a handful of fresh mint) · a squeeze of lemon (optional).

  1. Steep the ginger in hot water for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the peppermint and steep 3 more minutes.
  3. Add lemon and sip slowly after dinner.
Skip peppermint if you have heartburn/reflux. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding or take blood thinners or other medication, check with your doctor before drinking ginger tea every day.

Questions

My lower back feels uncomfortable on the towel.

Use a thinner roll, shorten the hold to 1 minute, and bend your knees with feet flat on the floor. If it still bothers you, stop and check with a health professional.

My arms or shoulders get tired overhead.

Keep your arms wider apart or slightly bent. You can also rest them on a pillow for the first few days.

I missed a day.

No problem. Just continue from the next day on your calendar. Consistency over perfection.

What do I do after day 21?

Keep 5 minutes a day with the towel plus the follow-up flow you liked most. If you got Phase 2, start it the next day.
