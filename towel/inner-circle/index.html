Before you start — please read. Talk to your doctor first if you are pregnant, gave birth in the last 12 weeks (or had a C-section), had recent abdominal or back surgery, have a hernia, osteoporosis, a diagnosed spine or disc condition, or any condition that makes lying on your back uncomfortable. A gentle stretch is normal. Stop right away if you feel sharp pain, numbness, tingling or dizziness.

Each month has a short routine, a simple challenge and a checklist. 3-month members: one new month every 30 days from your purchase. 1-month pass: Month 1.

Month 1

Morning Reset

A 6-minute routine to start your day:

  1. Towel 4 min at your spot
  2. Pelvic tilts × 10
  3. Cat–cow × 6
  4. Standing side stretch × 5 breaths each side
Challenge: The 20-of-30 challenge: do your Morning Reset on at least 20 of the next 30 days.

Checklist

Month 2

Strong Center

A 7-minute core routine:

  1. Towel 5 min
  2. Dead bug × 10/side
  3. Side plank from knees × 20 s/side
  4. Glute bridge × 15
Challenge: The posture challenge: 3 posture checks a day (sit tall, chin back, shoulders down) for 21 days.

Checklist

Month 3

Flow & Lightness

A 8-minute flowing routine:

  1. Towel 5 min
  2. Bird dog × 8/side
  3. Lying twist × 5 breaths/side
  4. Child's pose × 5 breaths
  5. Then a 10-minute walk after dinner
Challenge: The lightness challenge: a 10-minute walk after your largest meal on 25 of 30 days.
