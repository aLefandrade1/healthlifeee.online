Your Phase 2

Japanese Waist Sculpt · Phase 2

30 more days, picking up where day 21 ends

How it works30 days
Before you start — please read. Talk to your doctor first if you are pregnant, gave birth in the last 12 weeks (or had a C-section), had recent abdominal or back surgery, have a hernia, osteoporosis, a diagnosed spine or disc condition, or any condition that makes lying on your back uncomfortable. A gentle stretch is normal. Stop right away if you feel sharp pain, numbness, tingling or dizziness.

How Phase 2 works

  • Start the day after you finish the 21-day plan.
  • Every day: your towel session (5 minutes, same spot and position as in your plan) + that week's set.
  • Each week builds on the last. Total time: 8–12 minutes a day.
  • Days 29–30: wall test, check-in, and choose your maintenance routine.

Your 30 days

Week 1

Deepen

Towel 5 min + Corset Flow with 2 extra reps per move. Add the side-lying breath: lie on your side, knees bent, 5 slow breaths into your top ribs, each side.

  • Day 1 · Towel 5 min + Corset Flow+
  • Day 2 · Towel 5 min + Corset Flow+ · 10-min walk
  • Day 3 · Towel 5 min + side-lying breath
  • Day 4 · Towel 5 min + Corset Flow+
  • Day 5 · Towel 5 min + Corset Flow+ · posture fix
  • Day 6 · Towel 5 min + De-Bloat Flow
  • Day 7 · Rest day · walk 20 min
Week 2

Strengthen

Towel 5 min + Strength Set: side plank from knees 20 s/side · bird dog 8/side · glute bridge march 10 · dead bug 10/side. 2 rounds.

  • Day 8 · Towel 5 min + Strength Set
  • Day 9 · Towel 5 min + Strength Set · 10-min walk
  • Day 10 · Towel 5 min + De-Bloat Flow
  • Day 11 · Towel 5 min + Strength Set
  • Day 12 · Towel 5 min + Strength Set
  • Day 13 · Towel 5 min + De-Bloat Flow
  • Day 14 · Rest day · walk 20 min
Week 3

Stand Tall

Towel 5 min + Standing Set: wall sit 30 s · wall angels 10 · standing towel pull-apart 12 (hold the towel at both ends, pull outward, squeeze shoulder blades) · heel raises 15. 2 rounds.

  • Day 15 · Towel 5 min + Standing Set
  • Day 16 · Towel 5 min + Standing Set · 10-min walk
  • Day 17 · Towel 5 min + Strength Set
  • Day 18 · Towel 5 min + Standing Set
  • Day 19 · Towel 5 min + Standing Set · posture fix
  • Day 20 · Towel 5 min + De-Bloat Flow
  • Day 21 · Rest day · walk 25 min
Week 4

Integrate

Towel 5 min + your choice of two sets (Corset Flow+, Strength, Standing) back to back. Keep total time under 12 minutes.

  • Day 22 · Towel 5 min + Strength + Standing
  • Day 23 · Towel 5 min + Corset+ + Strength
  • Day 24 · Towel 5 min + De-Bloat Flow
  • Day 25 · Towel 5 min + Standing + Corset+
  • Day 26 · Towel 5 min + Strength + Standing
  • Day 27 · Towel 5 min + De-Bloat Flow
  • Day 28 · Rest day · walk 25 min
Days 29–30

Check-in & maintenance

  • Day 29 · Wall test + your favorite setCompare with your notes from the 21-day plan.
  • Day 30 · Choose your maintenanceKeep 5 minutes of towel + one set, 5 days a week.
